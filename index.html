Based on deep analysis of all 8 recent PYQs (May 24 to Sept 26), 6 MTP series, and the newly released Jan 2027 RTP.

🎯 1. Why Chapter 1 & 2 are Skipped Initially

Strategic Rationale: Chapters 1 & 2 (Preliminary & Incorporation) are traditionally over-studied by students, leaving them exhausted before reaching the high-scoring, marks-dense chapters (Ch 7 Management, Ch 10 Audit, Ch 9 Accounts, and Other Laws).

By starting with Chapter 3 (Prospectus) and moving directly through Capital, Deposits, Charges, Management, Dividend, Accounts, and Audit, you master 85% of exam weightage first. Chapters 1 & 2 are reintroduced in Week 7 (Dec 15-17) after you already understand shares, debentures, and meetings—making doctrines like Indoor Management and Ultra Vires 10x faster to absorb!

⏳ 2. The 3-Hour Daily Execution Formula (180 Mins)

Every single day is structured into three disciplined, scientifically proven blocks:

  • Block 1 (90 Mins) — Deep Concept Mastery: Focus strictly on Core Provisions, Section Numbers, Time Limits, and Exceptions. (Read 'What to Study', strictly skip 'What NOT to Study').
  • Block 2 (45 Mins) — Dual Question Practice: Solve targeted questions split into 👁️ REFER (absorb suggested reasoning) and ✍️ WRITE & PRACTICE (15-min handwritten drafting drill).
  • Block 3 (45 Mins) — Spaced Repetition Review: Review flashcards, time limits, or take the scheduled progressive mini-mock test.

  • Remittance of container detention charges exceeding the rate prescribed by Director General of Shipping.
Schedule III (RBI Approval beyond limits) PRIOR APPROVAL OF RESERVE BANK OF INDIA MANDATORY IF EXCEEDING SPECIFIED LIMITS
  • Individuals (LRS): Up to USD 250,000 per financial year for any permitted current/capital account transactions (travel, medical, gifts, education). Medical & education can exceed USD 250K if estimated by foreign hospital/institute.
  • Consultancy Services for Infrastructure Projects: Remittance EXCEEDING USD 10,000,000 (USD 10 Million) per project requires RBI approval. Up to USD 10 Million is freely permissible without approval!
  • Consultancy Services for Other Projects: Remittance EXCEEDING USD 1,000,000 (USD 1 Million) per project requires RBI approval.
  • Pre-incorporation expenses reimbursement: Exceeding USD 100,000 or 5% of investment brought into India, whichever is higher.
  • Donations for establishing Chairs in reputed educational institutes: Exceeding USD 5,000,000 or 1% of foreign exchange earnings during previous 3 FYs, whichever is lower.
  • Commission to agent abroad for sale of residential flats/plots in India: Exceeding USD 25,000 or 5% of inward remittance, whichever is higher.

4. Interpretation of Statutes: Latin Maxims & Drafting Clauses

Latin Maxim / Drafting Clause Literal English Meaning Judicial Application & Exam Significance
Contemporanea expositio est optima et fortissima in lege The best and surest mode of construing an instrument is to read it in the sense which would have been applied to it by the persons who were contemporaneously conversant with the circumstances. Long-standing administrative practice and contemporaneous executive usage of an ancient statute is respected by courts unless clearly contrary to text (Tested in Sept 26 Q4(c)).
Ejusdem Generis Of the same kind or species. Where specific words forming a distinct genus/class are followed by general words, general words are construed as limited to things of the same kind. Requires 5 conditions (enumeration of specific words, distinct class, class not exhausted, general words follow, no contrary intention).
Noscitur a Sociis A word is known by the company it keeps. The meaning of a doubtful word may be ascertained by reference to the meaning of other words associated with it in the same context.
Expressio unius est exclusio alterius The express mention of one person or thing implies the exclusion of other persons or things of the same class. When a statute specifically mentions certain items, items of the same class not mentioned are presumed to be excluded by the legislature.
Ut res magis valeat quam pereat It is better for a thing to have effect than to be void. Courts must lean towards an interpretation that validates the statute and makes it workable, rather than an interpretation that destroys it or renders it futile.
Non-obstante Clause ('Notwithstanding anything contained...') Has an overriding effect over specified provisions or other laws. Enforces legislative intent that the enacting section will prevail in case of any conflict with other sections (Tested in Sept 26 Q3(c)).
Without prejudice to... Does not limit, restrict or prejudice the generality of the other provision. Both provisions operate independently. Imposing a penalty under one section does not preclude suspension under another (Tested in Sept 26 Q3(c)).
